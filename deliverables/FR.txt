The system shall display study locations filtered by the available amenities* selected by the user

The system shall save the reservation details* specified by the customer 
The system shall display every reservation with its details* when the user is logged-in as a venue owner

The system shall send an email to a customer who made a reservation 1 hour before it starts, containing the address and the phone number of the venue 

Glossario
*Amenities: private parking spot, metro stop or bus stop within 500 meters, availability of drinks and food, free wi-fi and power outlets
*Details: number of people attending, date and time of the reservation, email of the student who made the reservation
